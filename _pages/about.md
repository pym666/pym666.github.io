I am Yanming Peng (彭彦铭), a second-year Ph.D. student in Cyberspace Science and Technology at Beijing Jiaotong University (BJTU), advised by Prof. Yaping Huang. I earned my B.E. in Computer Science and Technology at BJTU in 2024 and entered the doctoral program in September 2025 after one year of master's study.

## Research Interests

I study how computer vision models can learn reliably from noisy labels. In **gaze estimation**, I am interested in how errors in training labels affect a model's predictions on a new dataset. In [SeeTN](/publication/2026-06-SeeTN), we compare similarities among eye images with similarities among their gaze labels to identify annotations that may be inaccurate. I presented this work in an invited talk at the GAZE Workshop at CVPR 2026.

My broader interests include foundation models, multimodal learning, and embodied intelligence.
