I am Yanming Peng (彭彦铭), a second-year Ph.D. student in Cyberspace Science and Technology at Beijing Jiaotong University (BJTU), advised by Prof. Yaping Huang. I earned my B.E. in Computer Science and Technology at BJTU in June 2024. I began my master's studies there that September and entered the doctoral program in September 2025.

My research focuses on **computer vision**, particularly **noisy label learning** and **gaze estimation**. I study how models can learn reliably when labels are inaccurate or supervision is weak.

Before starting my current research, I took part in algorithmic programming competitions, including the [ACM-ICPC and CCPC](/awards/).

## Research Interests

- **Computer Vision**
- **Robust Learning**
- **Noisy Label Learning**
- **Gaze Estimation**

My broader interests include foundation models, multimodal learning, and embodied intelligence. I plan to explore some of these areas in greater depth.
