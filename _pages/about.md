I am Yanming Peng (彭彦铭), a second-year Ph.D. student in Cyberspace Science and Technology at Beijing Jiaotong University (BJTU), advised by Prof. Yaping Huang. I earned my B.E. in Computer Science and Technology at BJTU in June 2024. I began my master's studies there that September and entered the doctoral program in September 2025.

My research focuses on **computer vision**, particularly **noisy label learning** and **gaze estimation**. I study how models can learn reliably when labels are inaccurate or supervision is weak. My broader interests include foundation models, multimodal learning, and embodied intelligence.

Before starting my current research, I competed in programming contests, including the [ACM-ICPC and CCPC](/awards/).

## Research Interests

- **Computer Vision**<br>
  <small>Learning from images and combining visual information with other modalities.</small>
- **Robust Learning**<br>
  <small>Learning reliably from imperfect or weak supervision.</small>
- **Noisy Label Learning**<br>
  <small>Identifying mislabeled examples and reducing their influence during training.</small>
- **Gaze Estimation**<br>
  <small>Predicting where people look and generalizing across datasets.</small>
